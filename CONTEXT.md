# Quiet Meridian glossary

- **Fleet:** five ships occupying 17 distinct chart cells.
- **Chart:** a 10×10 sea grid, with rows A–J and columns 1–10.
- **Deployment:** the phase in which the captain places or moves ships.
- **Shot:** a single attempt at one previously untried rival cell.
- **Hit:** a shot at an occupied cell.
- **Miss:** a shot at an unoccupied cell.
- **Sunk:** every cell occupied by a ship has been hit.
- **Mission:** deployment followed by alternating shots until one fleet is sunk.
- **Shot report:** a revealed coordinate and its result, including cells of a newly sunk ship.
