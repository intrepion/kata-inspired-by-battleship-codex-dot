LOCAL PLAY / SOLO DUEL

THE CAPTAIN’S HANDBOOK

A little strategy.
A little sea luck.

  1. Deploy. Place five ships on your 10×10 chart: lengths 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2. Ships run horizontally or vertically, cannot overlap, and may touch. Select a placed ship to move it, or shuffle for a fresh arrangement.
  2. Search. You fire first. Click or tap a rival square. Then the rival takes one shot. A hit never grants an extra turn.
  3. Read the water. × marks a hit; • marks a miss. Every cell of a ship must be hit to sink it. Sink all five rival ships to win.

Keyboard: Tab into a chart, use arrows to move, Enter or Space to select. R rotates during placement. Tab reaches all other controls. Escape closes this handbook.

Your rival uses only previous shot reports to hunt. No peeking at your ships.
